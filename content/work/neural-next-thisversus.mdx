## The constraint

High-consideration purchases — cars, homes, enterprise electronics — have **23× more decision points** than simple buys. Buyers don't need another chatbot generating prose; they need structured comparison, constraint filtering, and explainable trade-offs across massive catalogs.

$412B in annual automotive revenue alone is lost to decision paralysis. The problem isn't information scarcity — it's decision architecture.

## The approach

ThisVersus is the decision layer between raw catalog data and the buyer:

- **Universal connector** ingests existing catalog JSON — zero schema migration
- **Neural core** vectorizes product attributes into a decision matrix
- **Generative canvas** renders interactive comparison UI per vertical
- Integration in **under two weeks**, SOC2-compliant

The tagline isn't marketing copy — it's the product thesis: *LLMs write text. We build decisions.*

## Verticals

- **Automotive** — OEM comparison portals with safety/price/budget axes
- **Real Estate** — property decision tools with constraint-based filtering
- **Electronics** — e-commerce engines for spec-heavy purchases

## Proof

Neural Next ships with engineering DNA from SoftBank, BNY Mellon, and IIM Bangalore. The platform is live at [neuralnext.co](https://neuralnext.co) with decision matrix demos across verticals.

For investors: this is a category play — decision infrastructure, not another RAG wrapper. For engineers: the hard problem is catalog-agnostic vectorization + explainable ranking, not prompt tuning.
