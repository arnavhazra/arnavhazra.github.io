## High-consideration purchases break on decisions, not information

Automotive alone loses an estimated $412B annually to decision paralysis. Buyers do not need another chatbot summarizing specs — they need constraint filtering, multi-axis comparison, and explainable trade-offs across catalogs too large to scan manually.

**23× more decision points** than a simple purchase. That is the gap Neural Next exists to close.

## What we built

ThisVersus is the decision layer between raw catalog JSON and the buyer:

- **Universal connector** — ingest existing catalog structure, no migration
- **Neural core** — vectorize attributes into a decision matrix
- **Generative canvas** — vertical-specific comparison UI (automotive, real estate, electronics)
- **Enterprise posture** — SOC2-compliant, sub-two-week integration

*LLMs write text. We build decisions.* That line is the product thesis, not marketing copy.

## Where it ships

Live at [neuralnext.co](https://neuralnext.co) with decision matrix demos across verticals. Engineering DNA from SoftBank, BNY Mellon, and IIM Bangalore informs how we think about enterprise delivery — not slide decks.
