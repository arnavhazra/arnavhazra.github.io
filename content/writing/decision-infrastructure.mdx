Most AI products in 2026 are content generators wearing enterprise costumes. Chatbots that summarize. Copilots that draft. RAG systems that retrieve and paraphrase. They optimize for **text output** and measure success in tokens generated.

That is a category error. The highest-value AI products structure **decisions**.

## What decision infrastructure means

Take raw catalog data — products, properties, specifications — and transform it into:

- Constraint-based filtering (budget, safety rating, location)
- Multi-axis comparison with explainable trade-offs
- Rankings that respect the buyer's criteria, not the model's fluency

The buyer does not read a paragraph about cars. They interact with a decision matrix.

## Why now

Three forces converged:

1. Catalogs grew past human comparison
2. Decision paralysis costs real revenue — $412B in automotive alone
3. LLMs can structure and explain, not just generate prose

The opportunity is not "add AI to your website copy." It is "replace the comparison page with a decision engine."

## What we built at Neural Next

ThisVersus ingests existing catalog JSON through a universal connector — **zero schema migration**, integration in under two weeks. The neural core vectorizes attributes; the generative canvas renders vertical-specific UI.

Automotive: safety × price × budget tier. Real estate: location × amenities × price range. Electronics: spec comparison with constraint filtering.

SOC2-compliant. Enterprise-ready. Not a demo — a deployment model.

Content generation is a feature. Decision infrastructure is a category.

LLMs write text. We build decisions.
